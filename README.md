# Sales Team Performance Dashboard — Q1 vs Q2 FY2026

Field-force business review comparing **Q1 (Jan–Mar 2026)** and **Q2 (Apr–Jun 2026)** for an 8-rep pharma sales team across Gujarat territories (Mehsana, Palanpur, Patan, Kadi). Currency: **INR (₹)**. Built from 15 Field Officer "Miss & Excess Call" reports.

## Headline results

| Metric | Q1 | Q2 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total business | ₹23.4L | ₹28.6L | **+22.4%** |
| Like-for-like (7 continuing reps) | — | — | **+7.5%** |
| Call adherence | 43.8% | 51.9% | **+8.1pp** |
| Calls made | 4,104 | 5,415 | +1,311 |
| Missed calls | 5,273 | 5,021 | −252 |
| Doctor coverage (Q2) | — | 38.7% | 473 of 1,223 active |

Top improver: **Bimal Bhavsar +60%** (₹292K → ₹467K, 5th → 2nd). Needs attention: **Parth Vyas** (−8%, still #1), **Kirit Bhadja** (lowest coverage 18.8%), **Mohammed Atik** (lowest yield).

## Notes & methodology

- Currency assumed INR (₹), inferred from Gujarat territories.
- Name spellings normalized across source files (e.g. Mohmmad/Mohammed Atik; Shaikh vs Sheikh).
- Ayush Vyas has Q2 data only (joined in Q2) and is excluded from like-for-like comparisons.
- Source month columns were stored out of calendar order; re-sequenced Jan → Jun.
- All figures reconciled against each source file's on-sheet summary box — 100% match.
